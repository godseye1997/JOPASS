Delete Your Account

How to permanently delete your JoPass account and data.

You can delete your JoPass account and all associated data at any time. There are two ways to do this.

Option 1 — Delete in the app (fastest)

  1. Open the JoPass app and sign in.
  2. Customers: go to the Profile tab and tap Delete Account.
  3. Venue owners: go to the Profile tab in the owner portal and tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm. Your account is deleted immediately.

Option 2 — Request by email

If you can't access the app, email us from the address on your account and we will delete it for you.

Send a message with the subject "Delete my account" to:
jopasscc@gmail.com

We will verify your request and complete the deletion within 7 days.

What gets deleted

When you delete your account, we permanently remove:

  • Your profile (name, email, phone number)
  • Your bookings and booking history
  • Your reviews, ratings, and followed venues
  • Your notification/device tokens
  • For venue owners: your venue listing, deals, and associated bookings

Some records may be retained for a limited period only where required by law (for example, financial or tax records of completed transactions). These are not used for any other purpose and are deleted when no longer required.

Questions

For any questions about account deletion or your data, contact jopasscc@gmail.com.
